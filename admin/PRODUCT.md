# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Platform administrators overseeing the client businesses that use Sales Engine.

## Product Purpose
Provide cross-business management and analytical visibility across outreach, Smart Leads, social listening, and CRM.

## Capabilities and Constraints
This repository is the admin frontend. The separate Sales Engine frontend serves client users and calls the Sales Engine backend. Admin CRM and social listening currently use illustrative data only: no APIs, provider connections, authentication, outbound messaging, or live CRM mutations. Demo management changes reset on refresh.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the existing Sales Engine branding and dashboard palette: deep teal canvas, white workspaces, wine activity surfaces, pale lavender supporting surfaces, and mint actions and active states. CRM follows the approved social listening visual language. Analytics should animate on scroll and display exact values on hover.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing admin dashboard and social listening implementation. Local client CRM schemas describe records, pipelines, assignments, contact details, notes, and activities. Fixture names are illustrative; financial values are not real results.

## Product Principles
- Make business ownership visible on every record.
- Explain analytic definitions and use consistent data across charts and tables.
- Record admin management decisions with reasons.
- Keep client sales execution distinct from platform oversight.
